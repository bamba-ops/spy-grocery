## Overview
This playbook compiles Marc Lou's landing page guidance from his newsletter posts and a YouTube community post. It focuses on what makes a landing page convert, using his own words as evidence.

## Principles
- Hook visitors with a promise that prevents the bounce. "The first job of your landing page is to stop people from closing the tab." — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Make the headline carry the conversion. "Your headline does 80% of the job" — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Use a bold promise that sparks curiosity. "The best headlines sound too good to be true" — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Prove the promise with ordered sections. "Each question your promise raises should be answered in its own section:" — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Show the product, not just copy. "An image is good. A video is better. A demo is best." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Keep copy tight and focused on proof. "Cut anything that doesn't help make the promise feel real. Extra words kill conversions." — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Iterate and refine based on reactions. "Great landing pages are built through iteration and repetition" — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Apply simple layout constraints. "3 design rules to make beautiful landing pages: 1024px max width, 16px horizontal padding, 80+% of the viewport height per section" — Post de Marc Lou - YouTube (accessed 2026-01-27) https://www.youtube.com/post/Ugkxair3WIpgCYD7-MWI5OSIhynW0UASS5cp

## What to do
- Answer the "why stay" question in the headline. "Why should a stranger stay on your site for more than 10 seconds?" — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Put the CTA in the hero and repeat it. "Don't bury your call-to-action button somewhere on the page." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Write benefit-first CTA copy. "Use this formula: Verb + Benefit." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Add social proof early. "If there's no social proof on your site, the conversion rate will plummet." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Collect testimonials before launch. "Get testimonials before you launch." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Show how it works in steps. "How does it work? -> Show steps 1, 2, 3." — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Show pricing to address affordability. "Can I afford it? -> Show pricing" — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Answer objections right under the CTA. "Answer objections in a small paragraph under the CTA:" — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Downplay branding to foreground the offer. "Make your brand name and logo smaller." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Remove clutter and show the key feature. "Remove clutter, especially if your product is complex." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts

## What to avoid
- Oversized branding in the hero. "I see websites with a massive 56px brand name and enormous logo in the center." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Safe, forgettable copy. "They write "clean" or "honest" copy that nobody remembers." — The Clickbait Landing Page (July 19, 2025) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/the-clickbait-landing-page
- Fake or suspicious proof. "Don't fake it at all." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Multiple competing CTAs. "Don't add other CTAs." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Generic CTA labels. "Avoid Get Started or Sign Up." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts
- Missing or absent headline. "Every site needs a headline (I've seen some without)." — How to write a landing page that sells (October 07, 2023) https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/how-to-write-a-landing-page-that-converts

## Hero section templates
- Template 1
  - Headline: "Get [primary outcome] in [timeframe] without [main pain]."
  - Subhead: "[Product] helps [audience] by [unique mechanism or proof]."
  - CTA: "[Verb + Benefit]"
- Template 2
  - Headline: "The [product category] that helps [audience] [achieve outcome]."
  - Subhead: "See how it works in [3 steps] and why it's different from [status quo]."
  - CTA: "[Verb + Benefit]"
- Template 3
  - Headline: "Stop [pain]. Start [benefit]."
  - Subhead: "Watch the [demo/video] to see [key feature] in action."
  - CTA: "[Verb + Benefit]"

## 1-page checklist
- Headline hooks and answers "why stay" in under 10 seconds.
- Promise is bold and curiosity-driven, then immediately proven.
- Sections answer objections in order: how it works, proof, pricing, urgency.
- Product demo is visible in the hero or right under it.
- CTA is repeated, benefit-based, and never buried.
- Social proof is real, specific, and close to the CTA.
- Branding is subtle; the offer does the talking.
- Copy is tight; every line supports the promise.
- Layout stays within 1024px, 16px padding, and tall sections.
- Iterate based on conversion feedback, not just traffic.
